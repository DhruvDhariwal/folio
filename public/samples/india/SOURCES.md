# Indian invoice sources

These are actual invoices published in public institutional records, not generated sample transactions. They are provided inside the private Folio prototype for document-review demonstration. No relationship with, approval from, or endorsement by the issuer or recipient is implied.

1. `turnitin-vignan-2023.pdf`: Turnitin India Private Limited invoice IN-TIN-01599, 26 July 2023, issued to Vignan's Foundation for Science, Technology and Research University. Extracted from page 10 of [Vignan's published supporting record](https://vignan.ac.in/aqardownload/3.4.1-SupportingDoc_23-24.pdf).
2. `turnitin-mit-einvoice-2024.pdf`: Turnitin India Private Limited e-invoice IN-TIN-02619, 23 April 2024, issued to MIT Art Design and Technology University Pune. Extracted from page 1 of [MIT-ADT's published supporting record](https://naac.mituniversity.ac.in/DVV/3_4_1/3_4_1_Bill_of_turnitin.pdf).

The source documents were retrieved on 1 November 2026. The one-page excerpts retain the original invoice identities, amounts, tax values and layouts. Banking instructions, personal account-manager contact details, a separately stated PAN, and handwritten endorsements have been removed where applicable. Each excerpt carries a privacy-edit notice. The WebP previews are renders of these exact excerpt PDFs.

No purchase order, authorized supplier list, or prior-obligation history for these real transactions has been supplied. Folio must request those records before any approval-ready result. The e-invoice QR, IRN and printed e-sign information are source content, not a claim that Folio verified the GST portal or a digital signature.

The five older USD scenarios are fictional and are labeled separately in the interface. Their procurement data must not be presented as real Indian purchasing records.
